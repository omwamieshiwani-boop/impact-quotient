Frequently Asked Questions

What does Impact Quotient actually do?

We help you define the change you want, choose practical indicators, gather evidence, learn from it and scale what works, for individuals, businesses and communities.

Do we need lots of data to start?

No. Most clients begin with very little. We start with a few meaningful indicators and build from there, focusing on quality of insight over quantity of data.

Is this only for large organisations?

Not at all. Individuals, small businesses, community groups and changemakers are central to who we serve. Our framework scales to your size.

How is this different from ESG or sustainability reporting?

Reporting describes what happened. We focus on learning and improvement, so reports become decisions, and decisions become better outcomes.
